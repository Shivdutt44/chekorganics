1. Placing an Order & Payment Options

Browse the CHEKORGANICS Online catalogue to make your purchase. When your order contains the items and quantities you desire, proceed through checkout. CHEKORGANICS Online is set up to accept immediate online orders through:

  • Domestic & International Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express).
  • Visa & RuPay Debit Cards.
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank UPI apps).
  • Net Banking across all scheduled Indian commercial banks.

Credit and debit card transactions are encrypted with 1024-bit SSL and authenticated via Verified by Visa (VBV) or MasterCard SecureCode (MSC). Credit Card statements will display the authorized billing entity Net Distribution Services Pvt. Ltd. / CHEKOTI BIO ORGANIC PRODUCTS LLP.

2. Cash on Delivery (COD) Terms

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on domestic orders above ₹3,000/- in serviceable pincodes across India.
  • If you are unavailable at delivery time, please communicate an alternative recipient or address to customercare@chekorganics.com within 48 hours of order placement.
  • If a COD order is returned due to customer unavailability or refusal, subsequent orders will only be fulfilled once converted to prepaid.
  • If a customer refuses COD delivery more than twice, COD eligibility will be permanently disabled for that account and phone number.
  • COD Refund Mode: Since cash is collected by 3rd-party logistics couriers, COD order returns cannot be refunded in physical cash. Approved COD returns are refunded as online store vouchers / credits redeemable across the store.

3. Order Cancellation Policy

If you wish to cancel an order, you must notify us within 24 hours of placing the order:

  • Email: customercare@chekorganics.com (mention Order ID in the subject).
  • Phone Helpline: +91-8374090068 (Mon–Sat, 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM IST).

Once orders have been batched and handed over to couriers, they cannot be cancelled. In case of cancellation prior to dispatch, a deduction of 2.5% will be applied towards gateway processing fees, and the remaining 97.5% balance will be credited to the original card or bank account in 7 to 10 working days.

4. Transit Damage, Leakage & Broken Product Claims

If you discover leakage or breakage upon delivery, please write to us within 48 hours of delivery date at customercare@chekorganics.com or WhatsApp +91-8374090068 with:

  1. Complaint Order Number & Invoice Number.
  2. Nature of the issue: (Leakage, broken seal, volume shortfall, wrong SKU).
  3. Clear photos or an unboxing video:
    • Clear photo of the unsealed/broken bottle or jar showing batch label.
    • Photo of the mono-carton (the branded individual product box).
    • Photo of the outer transit corrugated shipping box with courier label visible.

Our customer resolution desk will review and respond within 3 working days. Once approved, an authorization number and doorstep reverse pick-up will be arranged.

5. Product Quality Related Concerns & Doctor Review

In the rare event of a quality concern regarding formulation or consistency, please send an email to customercare@chekorganics.com within 7 days of receipt with your order ID, photos of the batch number printed on the base/back label, and a description of your concern.

  • Our team will connect with you within 2 working days.
  • Depending on the nature of the inquiry, a consultation with our in-house Ayurvedic Doctor / Quality Chemist is arranged.
  • If deemed necessary, the item will be recalled for farm laboratory assessment. The lab quality report is issued within 7 working days of receiving the sample.
  • If the product exhibits an authentic batch discrepancy, an immediate replacement or full refund is initiated.

6. Non-Eligible Items & Exclusions

  • Damages resulting from customer neglect, improper storage in direct sunlight, or incorrect application are not covered.
  • Due to strict hygiene, botanical sensitivity, and COVID-19 safety standards, used personal care items or products opened without batch defect cannot be returned based on subjective fragrance or personal color preference.
  • Special festive gift sets and limited-edition hampers are non-returnable once seals are opened.
  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery date without prior complaint authorization are not eligible for refunds.

7. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your returned consignment is inspected at our Hyderabad facility:

  • Online Prepaid Orders (Cards / Netbanking / UPI): The approved refund is automatically processed back to the original payment method within 10 to 15 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: The refund is credited as a CHEKORGANICS online store voucher code sent via email/SMS within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Late or Missing Refunds: If 15 business days have elapsed and your refund is not visible on your statement, please check your card provider first (as banks may take extra settlement cycles), then email us at customercare@chekorganics.com with your authorization ID.
