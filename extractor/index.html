    What a browser can and can't read

    This page has no server. It runs on GitHub Pages, so your browser fetches the store directly, and browsers only let a page read another site's data when that site sends CORS headers. Shopify sends them for its JSON feeds, but not for storefront HTML.

    Extracted

    • Products, variants, options, prices, availability, tags, SKUs
    • Barcodes, image alt text and product media (via products/{handle}.js)
    • Collections, collection images and the products in each collection
    • Pages (pages.json, where the store exposes it): HTML, clean text, headings, links, images
    • Every product, collection and page image, de-duplicated, with its source page
    • Store name, currency and published counts (meta.json), used to verify completeness

    Not readable from a browser

    • Homepage hero banners, posters and other theme section images
    • Blogs and articles
    • sitemap.xml and robots.txt
    • SEO titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, JSON-LD, review widgets
    • Policy pages (shipping, refund, privacy, terms)

    These are marked unavailable in every export. Nothing is guessed or filled in. Reading them needs a small server-side proxy, such as a Cloudflare Worker. Requests are rate-limited and retried with backoff, and nothing is sent anywhere except the store you enter. Extracted content stays the property of the store owner.
