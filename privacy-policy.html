Our Core Privacy Commitment:
• CHEKORGANICS (CHEKOTI BIO ORGANIC PRODUCTS LLP) will never rent, sell, or trade your personal telephone numbers or home addresses to third-party data brokers.
• Information collected is strictly utilized to process your organic orders, facilitate doorstep couriers, and provide customer assistance.

1. Personal Information We Collect

When you visit, create an account, or complete a checkout with CHEKORGANICS, we collect identifiable information required to fulfill your order safely:

  • Contact Details: Full name, shipping address, billing address, phone number, and email address.
  • Transaction & Order History: Items ordered, wishlist selections, order date, delivery instructions, and return/exchange records.
  • Payment Identifiers: Payment method selected (UPI, Credit Card, COD). Card numbers, CVVs, and banking passwords are processed through secure RBI-authorized payment gateways and are never stored on CHEKORGANICS servers.
  • Communications: Customer care inquiries, WhatsApp messages, order queries, and product reviews.
  • Device & Usage Information: IP address, device type, browser information, and visited pages to ensure optimal speed and prevent unauthorized checkout attempts.

2. How We Collect Your Information

  • Directly from You: When placing an order, filling our contact form, subscribing to harvest newsletters, or registering an account.
  • Automated Technologies: Through secure session cookies that keep items saved in your cart and track page responsiveness.
  • Courier Partners: Real-time status updates received from Shiprocket, Delhivery, or Blue Dart to provide you with delivery confirmations.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We use your personal data exclusively for lawful, legitimate business objectives:

  1. Order Fulfillment: To process payments, dispatch parcels from our Telangana farm/warehouse, send tracking alerts, and manage reverse pickups.
  2. Customer Support: To answer product inquiries, provide dosage/usage guidance from our team, and resolve delivery delays.
  3. Fraud Detection & Payment Security: To protect buyers against card fraud and verify Cash on Delivery orders.
  4. Regulatory Compliance: Maintaining GST sales invoices and audit documentation under Indian tax and corporate laws.

4. How We Share & Disclose Information

We only share minimal, requisite customer data with trusted partners under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Logistics Couriers: Name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with delivery personnel strictly for doorstep transit.
  • Payment Gateways: Encrypted transaction requests sent to Razorpay/Stripe/UPI providers for payment confirmation.
  • Legal Obligations: In response to official court orders or law enforcement directives in compliance with Indian jurisprudence.

5. Data Security & 1024-bit Encryption

All pages across CHEKORGANICS operate over HTTPS (Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure) with 1024-bit SSL encryption. All checkout sessions transmit via encrypted sockets, protecting against unauthorized eavesdropping. Look for the secure padlock icon in your browser's address bar during browsing and payment.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use standard functional cookies to:

  • Preserve items in your shopping bag between browsing sessions.
  • Remember your delivery PIN code preference across product pages.
  • Maintain secure login sessions for registered customers.

You can adjust or disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; however, disabling functional cookies may prevent the shopping cart from retaining your selected items.

7. Customer Data Rights & Access

Under applicable Indian and international privacy standards, you hold sovereign control over your data:

  • Right to Review: Request a summary of the contact and order details on file.
  • Right to Rectification: Update inaccurate delivery addresses or phone numbers.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your account profile and non-essential marketing records by emailing our data desk.
  • Opt-Out from Marketing: Click unsubscribe at the bottom of any promotional email or reply 'STOP' to promotional WhatsApp notifications.

8. Children's Privacy

CHEKORGANICS products are intended for purchase by adults over 18 years of age. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors.
