    Frequently asked questions

    How long does delivery take?

    Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery (₹149) is dispatched with priority and usually arrives in 1–2 business days. Set your PIN code with “Deliver to” at the top of the page to see an estimated date on every product.

    Is shipping free?

    Standard shipping is free on orders above ₹3,000. Below that, it costs ₹99.

    Can I pay Cash on Delivery?

    Yes. Choose Cash on Delivery at checkout. We confirm COD orders with you on WhatsApp before dispatching them, and you pay in cash or UPI when the parcel arrives.

    How do I pay by UPI?

    At checkout, scan our PhonePe merchant QR or tap “Pay on Mobile UPI App”, pay the amount shown, then enter the 12-digit UPI transaction ID (UTR). We start preparing your order once the payment is matched.

    How can I track my order?

    Use “Track Order” in the account menu with your order ID or mobile number. We also email you when your order is confirmed, dispatched, out for delivery and delivered, with courier tracking details once it ships.

    What if my product arrives damaged or wrong?

    Write to us within 7 days of delivery at hello@chekorganics.com (or reply to your order email) with your order ID and a photo, and we'll arrange a replacement.

    Do you have a first-order offer?

    Yes — use code WELCOME10 at checkout for 10% off your first order.
