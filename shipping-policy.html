1. Order Processing & Farm Batching

  • All orders are batched and processed within 1–2 business days (excluding Sundays and national public holidays).
  • Orders placed after 6:00 PM IST are processed on the subsequent working day.
  • During high-demand festival periods and seasonal harvests, dispatch may take an additional 24 hours to ensure fresh preparation.

2. Shipping Coverage

  • We deliver across all states and union territories in India through trusted courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC & Speed Post).
  • For bulk or international export orders (covering over 28 countries), please contact our corporate desk at hello@chekorganics.com.

3. Shipping Charges

  • Free Shipping: Automatically applied at checkout on domestic prepaid orders above ₹3,000.
  • Standard Shipping: A nominal flat fee of ₹90 applies on standard orders under ₹999.

4. Estimated Delivery Timelines

  • Metro Cities (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai, Kolkata): 3–5 business days.
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities: 4–7 business days.
  • Remote / Northeast / Island Locations: 5–8 business days.

Note: Delivery estimates may experience slight delays during extreme weather conditions or regional transport restrictions.

5. Real-Time Order Tracking

Once your parcel is batched and handed over to our logistics partner, you will receive an instant SMS and WhatsApp notification with your live tracking number. You can also view your live status on our homepage using the Track Order tool.

6. Cash on Delivery (COD) Guidelines

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on serviceable pincodes across India.
  • To protect against automated spam bookings, COD orders are confirmed via WhatsApp or phone call prior to farm dispatch.
  • Orders that remain unconfirmed after 48 hours may be cancelled automatically to prevent warehouse hold-ups.

7. Damaged / Tampered Packages Protocol

Crucial for Safe Claims:
• If the outer package is visibly crushed or torn at delivery, please refuse delivery OR immediately record a continuous unboxing video before breaking the inner safety seal.
• Report any transit damage or product leakage within 24 to 48 hours to customercare@chekorganics.com or WhatsApp +91-8374090068 along with photos/video.
• Verified claims receive a free reverse-pickup and immediate replacement!

8. Order Modifications & Cancellations

If you made an error in your delivery address or wish to adjust your items, please notify us within 12 hours of placing the order. Once the parcel is sealed and picked up by the logistics courier, in-transit cancellations cannot be processed.

9. Logistics Contact Desk

For any queries regarding delayed deliveries or address corrections:
